Help

Step-by-step instructions for new front-desk staff.

Logging in

  1. Enter your username.
  2. Enter your password.
  3. Click Login.

Registering an appointment

  1. Select New Appointment from the dashboard.
  2. Enter the patient's name, contact number and address.
  3. Enter a unique appointment number.
  4. Select a dentist.
  5. Select a treatment.
  6. Select the appointment date and time.
  7. Click Register Appointment.
  8. Note down the appointment number - the patient will need it for billing.

Finding an appointment

  1. Select Find Appointment from the dashboard.
  2. Enter the appointment number.
  3. Click Search.

Generating a bill

  1. Select Billing from the dashboard.
  2. Enter the appointment number and click Find Appointment.
  3. Click Generate Bill.
  4. Review the consultation fee, treatment cost and total.
  5. Click Print Bill to print the receipt.

If the dentist is already booked

If the system shows "This dentist is already booked at this date and time", choose a different time or a different dentist for that appointment.

Logging out

Click Logout at the top of any page when you are finished.
